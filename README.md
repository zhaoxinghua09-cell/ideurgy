## Definition / 定义

**EN**
Ideurgy is the engineering of ideation: generating creative candidates systematically through divergence, categorization, error-seeking filtering, convergence, and landing. Candidates are not conclusions; they must pass evidence, prior-art, and empirical validation.

**ZH**
创意工程术是创意的系统化生成：发散→分类→筛选（带找错）→收敛→落地。创意的候选必须过证据/查重/实测三纪律。
